## Product experience: one store, two modes
- **Quick Shop:** default fast, accessible storefront; search, filters, products, cart, checkout.
- **Virtual Showroom:** opt-in immersive boutique; category zones, mannequins, rose-gold racks, five garments per rack, in-place browse, front/back controls where real back photos exist.
- Both modes must use the same public catalog, inventory, cart, checkout and order data.
- Lazy-load showroom assets; avoid making Quick Shop depend on heavyweight scene assets.
- Showroom cashier must use the *existing* bank-transfer and automated slip-check workflow, not invent a credit-card payment integration.
- Branding: use authentic BADDIE B assets; circled character is **B**, not ® or R. Do not treat AI-rendered brand marks as official logos.
- Marketing slogans are placeholders pending owner approval.
